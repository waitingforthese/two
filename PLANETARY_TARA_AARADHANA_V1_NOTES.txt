LifeAlarm V14.2.2.4 BASELINE – Planetary Tara Aaradhana V1

BASELINE LOCK: V14.2.2.4 BASELINE – Gochar Button Fix

Feature:
- New Planetary Tara Aaradhana screen/button for all 9 grahas.
- Uses user's birth Nakshatra and current sidereal transit Nakshatra.
- Identifies Vipat, Pratyari and Vadha Tara for each transit graha.
- Shows current transit rashi, degree, nakshatra, pada, Tara, Nakshatra start/end.
- Shows next Vipat/Pratyari/Vadha window start when available.
- Independent master switch and independent per-graha switches.
- Alarm is scheduled at the start of the next warning Tara-nakshatra window.
- Alarm notification includes graha, Tara category and devotional mantra.
- Aaradhana voice uses the existing saved Japa count and speech rate.
- Added a section/button inside Nakshatra Aaradhana to open the planetary Tara screen.
- Added Home button below Gochar Analysis.
- Uses existing master alarm as the final global safety gate.

Important interpretation:
This is a traditional Tara-bala based devotional/observational feature, not a deterministic prediction of accidents or events.
