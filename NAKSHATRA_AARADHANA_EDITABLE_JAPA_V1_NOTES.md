# Nakshatra Aaradhana Editable Japa V1

Baseline: LifeAlarm_FRAMEWORK_USER_MANAGEMENT_V1_DETAILED_STUDY_POPUP_BIRTH_LAGNA_TABLE_V9_MASTER_ALARM.zip

Changes:
- Special Aaradhana japa count is editable per user/profile: 1–108, default 11.
- Special Aaradhana repeat interval is editable: 1–24 hours, default 1 hour.
- Aaradhana female/selected TTS speech rate is editable from 0.35 to 0.90; lower means slower/clearer. Default 0.72.
- Saved settings are profile-scoped through AaradhanaPrefs.
- Saving settings immediately reconciles the alarm schedule.
- The selected japa count is used for Nakshatra/Yoga/Karana change Aaradhana and Special Aaradhana.
- Master Alarm behavior remains unchanged.
